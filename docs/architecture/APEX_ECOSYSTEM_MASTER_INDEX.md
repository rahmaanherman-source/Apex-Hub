### APEX Vibe Translator

The canonical name for the previously described “APEX Universal Translator” concept.

Core principle:

> Hear the world in its own voice. Understand it in yours. Nothing gets lost in between.

Modes:

1. **Subtitles** — original audio untouched; translated meaning synchronized beneath it.
2. **Layered Voiceover** — original vocal remains audible while translated speech is layered above it.
3. **Full Dub** — translated voice replaces the original only when explicitly selected.

Translation preserves register, slang, rhythm, humor, attitude, cultural texture, and intent rather than flattening everything into neutral English.
