# APEX Creator Board — IP, Prior-Art & Licensing Brief

**Status:** IDEA BOX / DO NOT TREAT AS PATENT-PROTECTED
**Owner/source:** MAC / APEX project record
**Created:** 2026-08-14
**Purpose:** Preserve the current concept, identify prior art, define a low/no-cost protection path, and prepare a licensing/manufacturing outreach path without spending money prematurely.

## 1. Current concept

APEX Creator Board is a proposed compact, affordable, modular physical/digital creative workstation. The working concept combines:

- a compact base/work surface;
- illuminated tracing/lightbox capability;
- retractable/folding/expandable support or work-surface concepts;
- ability to accommodate a user-owned phone/tablet/foldable/portable display;
- physical paper, stencil, drawing, tattoo, pattern, blueprint and craft workflows;
- potentially continuous/large-format paper extending beyond the compact base;
- clips/magnets/retention features;
- image import from phone/cloud/camera and APEX/Gabby preparation of reference images;
- optional camera capture of physical work back into APEX;
- a software workflow in which Gabby can prepare, position, lock, scale, enhance, capture and organize reference/art assets;
- modular construction using affordable and, where structurally/safely appropriate, recycled materials;
- accessibility/customization possibilities such as contrast, brightness, color/visual settings and creator-specific configurations.

This document records the concept as currently understood. It does **not** assert that every element is new, patentable, or legally protected.

## 2. Prior-art signal found on 2026-08-14

Initial search found multiple earlier patents covering portions of the concept, including:

- US5217293A — transparent drawing board + light box, angled support and removable/rotatable board.
- US4450774A — drafting/drawing/tracing/light table with movable/rotatable work area and locking mechanism.
- GB2391167A / WO2004010824A1 — portable fold-up work surface capable of functioning as drawing board, light box, laptop surface and other work surfaces.
- US20220139276A1 — light-box construction with foldable/rollable components.

Therefore the project must **not** be marketed as simply “a new lightbox” or “a new folding drawing board.” The potential differentiator to investigate is the specific modular physical/digital combination, configuration, user-owned display integration, expandable/retractable work surface, APEX/Gabby workflow, capture/re-entry workflow, accessibility/customization, and any novel mechanical implementation that survives a professional prior-art search.

## 3. Evidence rule

APEX should preserve each meaningful iteration as a separate version. Never overwrite the original invention record.

Recommended evidence package:

1. dated concept description;
2. sketches/photos;
3. CAD/3D files;
4. source prompts and AI outputs where relevant;
5. human modifications and design decisions;
6. requirements/specification;
7. prototype measurements;
8. test results and failures;
9. prior-art sources reviewed;
10. Git commit/file history;
11. SHA-256 hashes for important exported artifacts;
12. final PDF invention disclosure package.

A GitHub commit provides a useful version-history record, but it is **not itself a patent filing and does not automatically establish patent rights or ownership**.

## 4. Lowest-cost legal/IP path to investigate now

A. Preserve the invention record privately in APEX/GitHub.

B. Avoid unnecessary public disclosure of detailed mechanisms until patent strategy is reviewed.

C. Run a structured prior-art search before making novelty claims.

D. Prepare an invention disclosure describing the specific new physical embodiments and software/physical interaction.

E. If eligible, pursue USPTO Patent Pro Bono assistance or a law-school IP clinic. USPTO states that qualified financially under-resourced inventors/small businesses may receive free patent legal assistance through its nationwide Patent Pro Bono Program. USPTO also provides a Pro Se Assistance Program for applicants filing without counsel.

F. If patent protection is appropriate, discuss a provisional application with a registered patent attorney/agent or qualified pro bono program. A provisional can establish a U.S. filing date for adequately supported subject matter, but new subject matter added later does not automatically receive the earlier date.

G. Maintain confidentiality/NDA controls for detailed disclosures to potential commercial partners until the IP strategy is set.

## 5. Monetization / licensing path

Do not manufacture at scale first.

Create a low-cost V0 proof of concept and a concise commercialization package:

- one-page product summary;
- problem/use cases;
- concept drawings;
- V0 prototype photos/video;
- functional requirements;
- prior-art boundary;
- differentiating features;
- target users;
- estimated target retail/manufacturing economics;
- requested partner role;
- licensing proposal structure.

Potential commercial structure to negotiate (not a predetermined entitlement):

- upfront license/option fee;
- continuing royalty based on defined sales/net sales or another negotiated metric;
- minimum annual royalty or minimum performance commitment where appropriate;
- field-of-use/territory limits;
- audit/reporting rights;
- milestone payments;
- reversion/termination provisions;
- ownership of improvements;
- confidentiality and IP representations.

WIPO confirms that licensing can be structured with lump-sum payments, royalties, or combinations of upfront payments and ongoing royalties. Exact economics must be negotiated and reviewed by counsel.

## 6. Outreach target

Do not send the entire invention package to random companies.

First identify companies that already manufacture or distribute:

- artist lightboxes/light tables;
- portable drawing boards;
- tablet/monitor stands;
- drafting/architectural workstations;
- tattoo/creative equipment;
- craft/DIY workstations;
- educational art equipment;
- portable/retractable work surfaces.

Approach them with a controlled teaser and request their IP/confidentiality process before detailed disclosure.

Suggested opening:

> We are developing a compact, modular physical/digital creative workstation that combines an expandable physical work surface, illuminated tracing/reference workflow, user-owned display integration, and a software-assisted capture/creation workflow. We are seeking a manufacturing/licensing partner to evaluate feasibility, prototyping, materials, and commercialization. Detailed technical disclosures are available subject to an appropriate confidentiality process.
