## 9. Funding source reconciliation

The supplied material contains several funding claims. Current external research changes the confidence level of some of those claims:

### NVIDIA Inception

NVIDIA currently describes Inception as a free startup program with no application fees, deadlines, or cohorts. Benefits include technical resources, preferred pricing, partner offers, investor exposure, and access to free cloud credits from NVIDIA partners. Specific credits are benefit-dependent; do not encode a guaranteed cash amount. [Official source: NVIDIA Inception]

### SBA Microloan

SBA currently states that Microloans are up to $50,000 through intermediary lenders. SBA says the average is about $13,000, lenders make credit decisions, and rates vary by intermediary. The supplied claim of a guaranteed 6–9% rate should therefore not be treated as canonical. [Official source: SBA]

### Kiva U.S.

Kiva currently advertises U.S. small-business loans from $1,000 to $15,000 at 0% interest, with no fees or collateral and no minimum credit score. The process includes application, community invitations, and public fundraising; approval and funding are not instantaneous. [Official source: Kiva]

### Microsoft for Startups

Current Microsoft documentation states that startups can receive up to $150,000 in Startup credits over time based on verified progress, service adoption, and sustained Azure usage. New applicants can start with $1,000 credits, with milestones leading to higher amounts. Investor Network-backed startups can have additional benefits. The old blanket statement “$150K immediate” is therefore not canonical. [Official source: Microsoft]

### Google for Startups Cloud Program

Current Google Cloud documentation states that eligible early-stage startups can receive up to $200,000 in Cloud credits, or up to $350,000 for AI-first startups under qualifying tiers. Eligibility depends on startup stage/funding and other requirements; the full amount is not universal. [Official source: Google Cloud]

### AI Grant

The current AI Grant site states a $250,000 uncapped SAFE plus $350,000 Azure credits and $250,000 additional credits, but it also states that Batch 4 applications are closed. Do not place it in an “apply today” queue without a new open application cycle. [Official source: AI Grant]

### NSF

The supplied material claims specific NSF SBIR Phase I amounts and eligibility. Those claims must be checked against the current NSF solicitation before being represented as an active application opportunity. This dossier intentionally does not mark the amount, approval probability, or eligibility as verified.

## 16. Source vs research notation

- **SOURCE:** supplied by the user or supplied document/package.
- **RESEARCH:** externally checked current information.
- **INFERENCE:** architectural interpretation derived from the source material.
- **UNVERIFIED:** claim requiring runtime/provider evidence.
- **VERIFIED:** backed by reproducible evidence in the repository/runtime.

This distinction is mandatory for future GODSPEED/APEX consolidation.
