# APEX LIFE GLOBAL — GABBY COMMERCE CO-HOST

**Status: OWNER-AUTHORIZED CONCEPT / IMPLEMENTATION SPEC**

## Goal

Use the canonical Gabby identity as a recognizable human-like APEX commerce co-host inside the APEX Life Global Hub/store.

This is a strong fit for the store because it turns the AI from a hidden utility into a branded host who can explain products and create a recurring shopping/media experience.

## Experience

Gabby should appear selectively in places such as:
- homepage hero/featured-product area
- new-arrival sections
- product spotlight cards
- promotional editorial sections
- live/recorded shopping segments
- product education modules
- podcast-to-store crossover segments

Possible presentation:
“Gabby’s Pick”
“Let Gabby show you”
“Gabby’s Product Spotlight”
“New at APEX Life Global”
“Great products. Let me show you why.”

## Interaction

A customer can:
- watch/listen to Gabby explain a product
- ask a question
- open the product
- compare products
- see supporting product information
- continue shopping

The storefront remains the store. Gabby is the host and guide, not a replacement for navigation or checkout.

## Content source

Use actual Shopify product data:
title, description, media, price, availability, variants and configured merchandising information.

Never invent:
- price
- inventory
- reviews
- discounts
- product specifications
- shipping claims
- medical/financial claims
- performance guarantees

## Frequency

Gabby should be **periodic and contextual**, not constantly animated or intrusive.

Do not place her over:
- checkout controls
- essential product controls
- navigation
- accessibility controls
- critical error messages

## Measurement

When analytics are available, track:
- Gabby impression
- spotlight opened
- play/listen started
- product click
- add-to-cart following Gabby interaction
- conversion following Gabby interaction
- dismissal
- repeat engagement

Analytics must distinguish observed data from inferred attribution.

## Identity law

The Gabby used here is the same canonical Gabby used by APEX Omni Studio and the podcast.

No separate Shopify avatar.
No separate podcast avatar.
No generic Sidekick mascot.

One Gabby. Different contexts.

## Rollout

1. Establish canonical identity asset.
2. Build storefront host component.
3. Connect real Shopify product data.
4. Add configurable placement/frequency.
5. Add voice/video/media support where actually available.
6. Test desktop/mobile/accessibility.
7. Verify product links and analytics.
8. Publish only after live verification.
