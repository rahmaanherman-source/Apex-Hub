# APEX Capability Audit

Identify the capability and authoritative documentation. Evaluate functionality, APIs, authentication, secret handling, cost, security, testability, and integration fit. Research first, test where authorized, record evidence, and never assign APPROVED or VERIFIED without supporting evidence.
